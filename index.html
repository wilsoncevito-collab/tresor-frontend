AudioForm

Available for Licensing
Modular · Integrates with Doubai & 3rd-party platforms

An intelligent and secure voice interface enabling automatic data entry and triage via voice command, eliminating language barriers. While natively integrated with Doubai for maternal health, its modular architecture allows rapid deployment on other platforms requiring offline multilingual voice recognition.

  • Medical & general speech-to-text (Whisper fine-tuned)
  • Real-time specialized terminology translation
  • Blockchain-secured consultation records
  • Open API architecture for third-party integration

Doubai

Available for Licensing
Natively integrates AudioForm technology

An AI-powered predictive platform for maternal and infant health, using transfer learning and contextual adaptation to forecast risks and optimize referrals. It natively integrates AudioForm technology, allowing caregivers to enter clinical data and receive voice alerts even in low-connectivity areas.

  • Transfer learning from global to African datasets
  • Contextual adaptation (distance, surgical capacity, access)
  • Cesarean risk prediction (0–100%) & urgency levels
  • Offline voice input & alerts via integrated AudioForm
